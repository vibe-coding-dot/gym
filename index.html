My Gym Plan 💪

Full-body, 3 days a week (4th day optional). Goal: get lean.

MonWorkout A
WedWorkout B
FriWorkout A
SatOptional: B or cardio

Next week, flip it: B / A / B. Rest day between sessions.

Finish every session: 10–15 min incline walk (treadmill, easy pace you can talk at).
Simple rules
  • Warm up 5 min (walk or bike), then do 1 light set of the first exercise.
  • Rest 60–90 sec between sets. Stop each set with about 2 reps left in the tank.
  • Hit the top of the rep range on all sets? Add the smallest weight jump next time.
  • Lean = lifting + a small calorie deficit + protein at every meal + 7–10k steps a day.
  • Pain (not burn)? Stop and drop the weight. Ask a trainer on the floor to check your form.
